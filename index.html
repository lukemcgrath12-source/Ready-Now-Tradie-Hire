Questions

Straight answers

Is it free?
Yes, for now. Listing your trade costs nothing and there's no card and no lock-in. Searching the directory and calling a tradie costs nothing either.
Are the tradies vetted?
No. Profiles are listed by the tradies themselves and Ready Now doesn't check licences, insurance or work history. Before work starts, ask for their licence and insurance and check them yourself. In NSW you can look up a licence on the NSW Fair Trading licence check.
How do I change or remove my profile?
There are no logins. Message Sam on 0432 910 369 and he'll update your photo, number or bio, or take you off the list.
Who do I pay?
You deal with the tradie direct. Price, timing and payment are between you and them. Ready Now isn't part of the job and doesn't handle payments.
What areas do you cover?
Wider Sydney. Each profile shows the suburb or area that tradie works, so search by your suburb to find someone close.
Something wrong with a profile?
Use the "Report this profile" link on the profile, or call Sam on 0432 910 369.
Can I hire equipment?
Not yet. Equipment hire is coming soon. Get in touch if you've got gear you'd like to list.
