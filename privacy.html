Privacy Policy

What we collect when you book a tradie or get in touch, why we collect it, and what we do with it. Plain English, no legal padding.

Last updated 17 August 2026

01Who we are

This website is operated by Ready Now Tradie Hire (ABN 38 659 734 077), a labour hire business supplying qualified tradies, apprentices and labourers across wider Sydney.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" means Ready Now Tradie Hire. "You" means anyone who visits this website, submits a booking request, or contacts us about labour hire.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

02What we collect

Information you give us

When you send a booking request, fill in an enquiry form, call us, or message us, we may collect:

  • Your name and the business or company you work for
  • Phone number and email address
  • Site address or job location
  • The trade you need, dates required, duration, and job details you choose to share
  • The specific worker you have requested, if you have picked one
  • Anything else you include in a message to us

Information collected automatically

When you browse this site, our hosting and analytics tools may record:

  • Your IP address and rough location, at city or region level
  • Browser and device type
  • Pages you viewed, how long you stayed, and the site or ad you arrived from

We don't collect sensitive information through this website — no health records, no financial details, no government identifiers. Please don't send them through the forms on this site.

03Why we collect it

We only collect what we need to run the business. Specifically, to:

  • Respond to your request and confirm your booking
  • Match the right tradie, apprentice or labourer to your job
  • Contact you about a job in progress or a booking you have made
  • Invoice you and keep the business records we are required to keep
  • Understand which pages and ads work, so we can improve the site
  • Send you updates about our services — only if you have asked for them

If you would rather not give us this information, that is fine — but we may not be able to confirm a booking or get someone to your site.

04Cookies, analytics and advertising

This site uses cookies and similar technologies. Some are necessary for the site to work. Others help us measure performance and reach the right people with advertising.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools such as Google Analytics to see how the site is used. These report on visitor behaviour in aggregate — we do not use them to identify individual visitors.

Advertising and remarketing

We may use advertising tools such as the Meta Pixel and Google Ads tags. These place a cookie in your browser so we can measure whether an ad led to a booking, and show our ads to people who have visited this site before.

These platforms handle data under their own privacy policies:

  • Meta Privacy Policy and Meta ad preferences
  • Google Privacy Policy and Google ad settings

Turning cookies off

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking them will not stop you using the site, though some parts may not work as smoothly.

05Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. Ever.

We share it only where it is needed to do the job:

  • Our workers — the worker assigned to your job gets the site address, contact name and number, and the job details. Nothing more.
  • Service providers — the tools that run this business, including our website host, form provider, email provider and accounting software. They can only use the information to provide their service to us.
  • Professional advisers — our accountant or lawyer, where required.
  • Where the law requires it — for example a court order, a regulator, or a workplace health and safety investigation.

Some providers store data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure the information is handled to a standard consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

06How we store and protect it

Your information is held in password-protected business systems with access limited to people who need it. We use reputable providers with their own security controls in place.

No system connected to the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

We keep personal information only as long as we need it, or as long as we are legally required to keep business records. After that, we destroy it or permanently de-identify it.

07Accessing, correcting or deleting your information

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Give you a copy of it
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date
  • Delete it, where we are not required to keep it
  • Stop sending you marketing messages

Email us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge, and we may need to confirm who you are before we release anything.

08Third-party links

This site may link to other websites — our Instagram page, for example. We are not responsible for how those sites handle your information. Check their privacy policies before you give them anything.

09Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first using the details below. Tell us what happened and we will investigate and respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled it, you can take the complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner:

  • Web: oaic.gov.au
  • Phone: 1300 363 992

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the business changes or the law changes. The current version always sits on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top. If we make a significant change, we will make that clear on the site.
